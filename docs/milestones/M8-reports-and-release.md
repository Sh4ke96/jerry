## What it changes in daily use

- **Final report.** Every task has one, in the dashboard ("Final report"), with `jerry report <id>`, or through
  MCP. It is built from what JERRY recorded, and is a draft until DONE.
- **Artifact.** After DONE, `/jerry:artifact` asks whether you want one, and which kind. Only on yes does
  Claude create it, as a private page in your claude.ai account. JERRY keeps your decision and the link.
  `/jerry:plan` asks up front too.
- **Releases.**
  - `pnpm release` produces `dist/release/jerry-0.1.0.tar.gz` with checksums and release notes.
  - Installing a newer package updates JERRY; installing the previous one rolls it back. Your data stays in
    both directions.
- **Security audit.** It lists what JERRY does and does not protect against, and three hard-rule decisions
  that are yours.

## Decisions waiting for the product owner

   user-only commands.
3. Narrow the false positives of `hard.core-api` and `hard.git-mutation`.
